= Qualified support and trust snapshots

The graph ranks the subgraph supported by outcomes accepted by
`OutcomeTracker.qualified_outcome?/1` and carrying a positive success edge.
Recipe inventory alone is not successful evidence. A node with no such edge,
including an entity observed only in qualified failures or false positives,
keeps the existing neutral score of `0.5`. It contributes no PageRank mass and
does not change normalization of the supported subgraph.

These are relative ranking scores, not calibrated success probabilities,
cryptographic attestations or authority to dispatch. Negative outcomes remain
in the graph's observations/nodes but do not create positive edges. A separate
negative penalty and time decay are still unimplemented; this change does not
claim that mixed success/failure histories have a calibrated risk score.

== Startup and persistence

The coordinator rebuilds the graph from the currently selected runtime data
store at startup and every learning cycle. It never overlays saved trust
scores. Legacy, current-labelled, malformed and future-version snapshots have
no restore authority. This is necessary even for structurally valid snapshots:
their outcomes may have been removed, invalidated or requalified since saving.

New trust snapshots use `schema_version: 2` and
`restore_policy: recompute_from_current_qualified_outcomes`. Their scores and
edges remain useful diagnostic history. The label does not authenticate them
or enable hydration. Old files are retained until ordinary persistence replaces
them; no migration deletes or edits the historical outcome corpus. Learned
network restoration outside the trust graph retains its existing behavior.

Both graph loading and neural snapshot persistence use `Hypatia.Paths` to
resolve the current runtime store. Changing that configured store therefore
changes the source read by the next rebuild and the destination of new snapshots.

== Qualification controls

`test/graph_of_trust_qualification_test.exs` exercises empty and unqualified
histories, failure-only and false-positive-only histories, and an expansion by
500 unsupported recipes. The positive-support ranking must survive unchanged
while all unsupported nodes remain neutral.

`test/neural_trust_restart_test.exs` calls the actual coordinator initializer
against synthetic poisoned snapshots and a replacement runtime store. It checks
that an old score cannot return under a current schema label, that persistence
writes the diagnostic version, and that a new qualified success still affects
the rebuilt graph. Fixtures live only in the disposable test store.

These tests qualify these source-level boundaries. They do not authenticate
the historical outcome corpus or establish a live dispatch/training deployment.
