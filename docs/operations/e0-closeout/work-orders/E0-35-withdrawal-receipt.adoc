== Goal

A withdrawal of an outcome row appends a receipt with the verdict `withdrawn` whose parent is the receipt of that row, so that the receipt log shows the withdrawal and the history is kept.

== Authority

Owner rulings of 2026-11-21 on `docs/design/outcome-retraction.adoc`, section 8a. Question 4, answer A: "the revision receipt records the withdrawal." The design is `docs/design/e0-receipt.adoc`, D7 (a revision is a new receipt with its own `event_id` and a `parent_event_ids`),
and the model of `E0-10` (`src/Hypatia/ABI/Receipt.idr`, P3: an append keeps every receipt before it).

== Facts measured

Read on 2026-11-21. Measure them again.

* `lib/hypatia/receipt.ex`: `build/2` always sets `parent_event_ids` to `[]` and takes the verdict from the gate. No run-time code builds a revision, a fill or a withdrawal (the mirror test says so).
* `schema/receipt.ncl`: a verdict `withdrawn` is accepted only with a parent.
* The writer refuses a duplicate `event_id` (`E0-29`).

== Steps

. Measure the facts again. Stop if one no longer holds. Confirm that `E0-07` is merged.
. Write the tests first: (a) a withdrawal appends 1 receipt with the verdict `withdrawn`, a new `event_id`, and `parent_event_ids` equal to the `event_id` of the receipt of the withdrawn row; (b) the receipt of the row is still in the log, unchanged;
  (c) the new receipt passes the Nickel contract (`nickel` on `PATH`); (d) a row with no receipt (a legacy row) gets no revision receipt and the withdrawal still succeeds, with a log line that says why;
  (e) the mirror test of `E0-10` is extended: the appended revision keeps every receipt before it.
. Add `Receipt.build_withdrawal/2` and call it from the withdrawal of `E0-07`.

== Acceptance

* The new tests pass, and they failed before step 3. Give both outputs.
* `bash scripts/check-verdicts.sh` still passes. `mix test` has 0 failures. No file that was formatted before the change is unformatted after it.
* The ledger rows of P3 and P4 name that a revision now has a run-time counterpart, and `scripts/proof-ledger.sh check` and `render --check` pass.

== Stop and ask

* A withdrawal receipt needs a field that the contract does not give.
* The cost reaches the cap before the acceptance checks pass.
