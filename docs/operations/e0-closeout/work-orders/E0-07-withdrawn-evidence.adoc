== Goal

When a person withdraws an outcome row, the confidence and the dispatch tier of its recipe are computed again without that row, and a finding that
cited the row stops resting on it. The withdrawn row stays in the log.

== Authority

https://github.com/hyperpolymath/hypatia/issues/984[Issue 984], step 9 ("If evidence is withdrawn, recompute or revise dependent verdicts") and acceptance A7. Related: https://github.com/hyperpolymath/hypatia/issues/919[issue 919].
Owner rulings of 2026-11-21 on `docs/design/outcome-retraction.adoc`, section 8a. The design is the owner's answers in `docs/design/outcome-retraction.adoc` and the design note of the stop of 2026-11-15, now ruled:

* A withdrawal calls `Quarantine.retract_dependents/1`, which now retracts the assumptions of the withdrawn outcomes as well (question B of the stop, then the extension of `E0-32`).
* The local id that `E0-09` gives a row is enough, and a row with none has the derived id of `E0-34`.
* Withdrawing a failure row may raise a tier (ruling 4A): a person withdrew it, and the log keeps both rows.

== Facts measured

Commit `4d7f059`, read on 2026-11-14, and again at the stop of 2026-11-15. Measure them again.

* `lib/outcome_tracker.ex`: `qualified_outcome_counts/1` has no notion of a withdrawn row. `update_confidence_file/5` computes confidence from the rows with `bayesian_update/3`.
  `annealed_strategy_from_store/3` and `reconcile_annealing_state/2` compute the stage from the qualified counts, so a new count can give a new confidence and a new stage.
* 5 readers call the gate for each row: `lib/cross_repo_learning.ex`, `lib/neural/training_pipeline.ex`, `lib/neural/graph_of_trust.ex`, `lib/neural/rebalancer.ex`, and the counts in `lib/outcome_tracker.ex`.
* `lib/confidence_annealing.ex`: a recipe is `:nascent` from 0 qualified outcomes, `:adolescent` from 5, `:mature` from 15, `:veteran` from 50. The tier of `:nascent` is `:report_only`, and the tier of `:adolescent` is `:review`.
* `lib/safety/quarantine.ex`: `retracted_assumptions/0` reads the quarantine table, and `retract_dependents/1` applies it. No module of `lib/` calls `retract_dependents/1`.

== Theory inputs

Apply a withdrawal to the log, then compute again. Do not correct the stored confidence (echo-types `EchoAggregation.agda`, line 210; `EchoSelectiveProjection.agda`, line 106). Check each row against the log that remains, in 1 place
(residual-evidence-types `Revision.agda`, lines 180 to 191). Not proved anywhere: that retraction is idempotent or free of order, and any statement on confidence.

== Steps

. Measure the facts again. Stop if one no longer holds. Confirm that `E0-34` and `E0-32` are merged.
. Write the tests first (they must cross a stage threshold):
.. 5 verified successes take a new recipe from `:nascent` to `:adolescent`, and its tier from `:report_only` to `:review`;
.. a withdrawal of 1 of the 5 returns the confidence, the stage and the tier to the values after 4 rows;
.. the log still has the 5 rows, and the withdrawal store has the record;
.. a withdrawn failure row raises the confidence of its recipe, and the stage and tier follow the counts;
.. the 4 other readers of the gate (cross-repo learning, the training pipeline, the graph of trust, the rebalancer) do not count a withdrawn row (1 test for each);
.. `Quarantine.retract_dependents/1` retracts a finding whose token cites the withdrawn row, and keeps one that cites another;
.. a withdrawal of a row that does not exist, or of an id that 2 identical legacy rows share, returns an error and writes nothing.
. Add the withdrawal store (`withdrawals/YYYY-MM.jsonl` under the data root, one canonical JSON object for each line: the id, the reason from a closed set, `by`, a time), a single loader in `OutcomeTracker` that
  reads the outcome rows and removes the withdrawn ones, and use it in the 5 readers. Compute the confidence and the annealing state again from that loader, and make `Quarantine.retracted_assumptions/0` include
  `{:outcome, id}` for each withdrawn row.
. Add `mix hypatia.withdraw_outcome --id ID --reason REASON --by NAME`, named in its usage text. No dispatch or scheduler path calls it.
