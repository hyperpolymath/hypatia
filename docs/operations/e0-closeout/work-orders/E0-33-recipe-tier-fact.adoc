== Goal

Each eliminate step of the dispatch manifest carries a second fact: the stage and tier of its recipe, as a `:threshold` claim that names the set of qualified outcomes behind it by a digest. When a row of that recipe is withdrawn,
a fact built before the withdrawal is retracted, and one built after it survives.

== Authority

Owner rulings of 2026-11-21 on `docs/design/outcome-retraction.adoc`, section 8a. Question 2, answer B (a first citer now: the recipe-tier fact) and question 3, answer B (cite a digest of the recipe's rows; any withdrawal of one of them invalidates the fact).

== Facts measured

Commit `689897b4`, read on 2026-11-21. Measure them again.

* `lib/dispatch_manifest.ex`, `facts_for/1`: the fact of a step is built from the pattern registry (`support` `observed` or `pattern`, with 1 evidence token that names `pattern_registry/<id>`). It cites no outcome row.
* `lib/confidence_annealing.ex`: the stage and tier of a recipe are functions of its qualified outcomes.
* A fact that listed every counted row would be as large as the count (a `:veteran` recipe has 50 or more). A digest is a fixed size.

== Steps

. Measure the facts again. Stop if one no longer holds. Confirm that `E0-07`, `E0-32` and `E0-34` are merged.
. Write the tests first, in `test/dispatch_manifest_test.exs` (each must fail today): (a) an eliminate step for a recipe that has qualified outcomes carries a `recipe_tier` fact with `claim: "threshold"`, the `recipe_id`, the stage, the count, and `outcome_set_digest`;
  a step for a recipe with none carries no such fact; (b) the digest is the SHA-256 of the sorted row ids of the recipe's qualified, non-withdrawn rows (the ids of `E0-34`); (c) the fact holds a digest and no list of ids;
  (d) after a row of that recipe is withdrawn, a fact built before is retracted by `retract/3` (digest check) and a fact built after the withdrawal survives; (e) a withdrawn row of another recipe changes nothing; (f) the other facts of the step are unchanged.
. Add the `recipe_tier` fact to `DispatchManifest`, and the digest function to `OutcomeTracker`. Do not change the strategy of a step: this is a fact, not a gate.

== Acceptance

* The new tests pass, and they failed before step 2. Give both outputs.
* `mix test` has 0 failures. No file that was formatted before the change is unformatted after it. `bash scripts/check-readme-claims.sh` passes.
* The pull request body says that no module reads the `recipe_tier` fact yet, and what a reader would do with it.

== Stop and ask

* The cost reaches the cap before the acceptance checks pass.
