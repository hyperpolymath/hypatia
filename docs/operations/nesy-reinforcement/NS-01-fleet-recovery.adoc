= NS-01: Fleet receipt and derived-state recovery
:toc:

Date: 2026-11-23. Baseline: `d158aed87f76b93e4f291e995bef9f26559c7fb2`.
Scope: recovery of already committed fleet outcome rows by the existing
LearningScheduler. This is a bounded reinforcement of the E0 consumer path,
not an E0 closeout or a new Kautz classification.

== Demonstrated failure

Fleet import appends the destination outcome before writing its receipt and
derived annealing/recipe state. Those later writes can fail without undoing the
committed outcome. The fleet signature cache and persisted import IDs then
correctly suppress re-import, but the local scheduler reader also discarded the
fleet row's recipe ID. A fleet-only workload therefore had no path selecting
that recipe for repair, including after a scheduler restart.

Selecting the recipe exposed a second failure: successful scalar recomputation
hid a failed receipt repair, so the scheduler dropped pending work after its
destination cursor advanced. The regression demonstrated both failures before
the corresponding fixes.

== Change

* The local reader retains fleet recipe IDs for reconciliation without counting
  their events again. Imported-event counts and reconciliation targets remain
  separate.
* `OutcomeTracker.update_recipe_confidence/1` captures one store for receipt
  repair and derived recomputation. A retryable receipt error remains an error
  even when scalar persistence succeeds; recomputation still runs.
* `Receipt.repair_with_status/2` distinguishes written, pending and refused
  receipts. The existing `repair/2` tuple remains compatible: its failed count
  includes both pending failures and final refusals.
* The existing final reasons, `duplicate_event_id` and
  `verified_without_evidence`, are shared with `report_failure/2`. They remain
  visible refusals and do not alone keep a recipe pending. Other failures retain
  the existing pending classification. No evidence or receipt is invented.

No event ID, qualification rule, fleet quarantine or neural learning guard is
relaxed. Existing fixed-prior confidence and annealing reconciliation perform
the recovery; repeated computation is allowed, repeated evidence credit is not.

== Executed evidence

All runs used `MIX_ENV=test`, Elixir 1.19.4, Erlang/OTP 27.3.4.19 and seed 0.
The scheduler fixture uses actual GenServer cycles, temporary outcome/recipe/
receipt/fleet stores, disabled service and peer polling, and a fresh temporary
action-SHA cache. Application-owned scheduler/anomaly timers are suspended and
the neural coordinator is unavailable during each fixture, then restored.

The actual scheduler controls cover:

* Unchanged fleet bytes, an advanced destination cursor, and a receipt path
  still blocked when scalar recomputation succeeds. Removing the obstacle
  repairs the receipt from pending work.
* Receipt and annealing write failures followed by a fresh scheduler whose
  cursor, signature and pending set are empty. The fleet-only committed row and
  persisted producer ID suffice to recover without manual recipe selection.
* Repeated polls preserve outcome, receipt, recipe and annealing bytes, with one
  qualified observation, no duplicate append and no additional reheat.
* Unverified/no-op audit rows gain receipts without positive or negative credit;
  start rows are skipped. Audit-only recipes acquire no annealing evidence.
* A final refusal and a retryable I/O failure on the same recipe remain distinct.
  The valid receipt recovers, the refused row and warning remain, and a later
  idle poll neither repeats the refusal nor recomputes the recipe.

== Claim boundary

These are controlled write-failure and fresh-process tests. They establish
recovery and idempotent evidence credit for the tested consumer paths. They do
not prove filesystem power-loss durability, an atomic multi-file transaction,
concurrent-writer safety, exactly-once neural parameter updates, or fleet
producer conformance. Scheduler diagnostic counters remain process-local.

Final refusals can be reported again on restart or when new outcomes select the
recipe. Rows without event IDs keep the existing no-backfill behaviour. Other
invalid evidence or an unregistered recipe may require intervention; not every
pending entry is guaranteed to clear. The focused run's three skips are
recorded above; the subsequent combined run executed those Nickel checks.
Neither run establishes production rollout or the excluded external-data
and integration capabilities.
